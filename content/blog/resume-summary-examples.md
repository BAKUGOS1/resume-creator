The summary sits at the top of your resume, exactly where recruiters look first. In [Ladders’ eye-tracking research](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ladders-updates-popular-recruiter-eye-tracking-study-with-new-key-insights-on-how-job-seekers-can-improve-their-resumes-300744217.html), the best-performing resumes opened with a clear overview of the candidate. Two or three sentences are enough.

## The formula

1. **Who you are:** your role and experience level, in the words of the job you want.
2. **What you’re good at:** two or three strengths or skill areas that match the posting.
3. **Proof:** one concrete result, scope or specialty.

Write it in plain sentences, without “I” — just start with the role.

## What to avoid

- Clichés with no evidence: “hard-working team player with excellent communication skills”.
- An objective statement about what _you_ want (“seeking a challenging role…”).
- A paragraph longer than four lines.
- Skills that don’t appear anywhere else in the resume.

## 12 examples

### Software engineer

> Backend engineer with 6 years building payment and platform services in Go and TypeScript. Designs reliable APIs and owns them in production; led a checkout redesign that cut p95 latency by 42%.

### Full-stack developer (early career)

> Full-stack developer who ships web and mobile products end to end: interface, API, database and automated tests. Built and launched a React and Supabase app now used by active customers.

### Data analyst

> Data analyst with 4 years in e-commerce, turning messy sales data into decisions. Fluent in SQL, Python and Power BI; built the weekly margin dashboard used by 40 category managers.

### Product designer

> Product designer focused on complex B2B workflows. Runs research, prototypes and ships with engineering; redesigned an invoicing flow that reduced support tickets by a third.

### Product manager

> Product manager with 5 years in fintech, leading discovery and delivery for teams of 6–10. Launched a lending product that reached 20k active users in its first year.

### Sales executive

> B2B sales executive with a record of closing mid-market SaaS deals. Exceeded quota in 7 of 8 quarters; strongest in consultative selling to finance and operations teams.

### Accountant

> Chartered accountant with 5 years in statutory audit and GST compliance. Leads month-end close for a 3-entity group and cut close time from 8 days to 5 through automation.

### Customer support

> Customer support specialist handling 60+ tickets a day across chat and email. Maintains a 94% satisfaction score and writes help-centre articles that deflect repeat questions.

### Marketing

> Performance marketer managing ₹50L+ monthly spend across Google and Meta. Grew qualified leads 2.3× in a year while lowering cost per lead by 28%.

### Teacher moving into instructional design

> Educator with 8 years designing curricula for 300+ students, now focused on instructional design. Builds clear, measurable learning experiences and is certified in Articulate 360.

### Career changer into tech

> Former operations lead moving into software development. Three years of accounts and ERP experience plus a portfolio of full-stack projects, including a CRM with spreadsheet import and deduplication.

### Fresher / recent graduate

> Computer applications graduate with internship experience in web development. Built three production-style projects with React and Node.js and enjoys turning business workflows into software.

## Tailor it for each application

Swap the role title and the two strengths to match the job posting’s wording. It takes two minutes and makes the top of your resume read as if it was written for that job — because it was.
